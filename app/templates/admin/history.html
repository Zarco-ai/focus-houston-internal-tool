Import earlier sheets

Loads months from the tracking spreadsheet into the app, exactly as they were recorded, so every report has them. Each row's count and dollars come in as the sheet had them; the app decides which report row they feed. Anything with no value or no category waits in the review queue for you to finish.

Everything goes in at once, or nothing does. The same file twice changes nothing. Card numbers aren't in the sheets, so retention and the other Program Health numbers start from the app's own intakes and form check-ins.

Save every session

Downloads every approved session exactly as it is now: every count, price and category you've set, and which lines are still in the review queue. Keep it somewhere safe. It's also how the sessions move to another copy of the app: import it here, in a copy that doesn't have those months yet. It contains your session notes, so keep it out of email and chat.
